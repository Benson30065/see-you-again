01 / THE BEGINNING

How the Song Came Together

After the loss of Paul Walker, the filmmakers needed music that could carry the emotion of the ending of Furious 7. The result was a song built around remembrance, friendship and family.

01

A Powerful Idea

Charlie Puth helped create the melody and emotional foundation of "See You Again", giving the track a reflective feeling that suited the film's goodbye.

02

Adding The Rap

Wiz Khalifa brought the rap verses, giving the song a second voice and connecting its message to friendship, memories and moving forward.

03

Made For The Ending

The track was selected for the closing sequence of Furious 7, turning the final scene into a musical farewell to Brian O'Conner.

04

More Than A Soundtrack

Its message reached beyond the movie. "See You Again" became strongly associated with remembering Paul Walker and the themes of family and friendship.

02 / THE ARTISTS

Charlie Puth × Wiz Khalifa

Two different musical styles came together to create one of the most memorable songs connected to the franchise.

SINGER • SONGWRITER • MUSICIAN

Charlie Puth

Charlie Puth contributed the song's vocal and songwriting side. His piano-led musical idea helped give "See You Again" its emotional identity.

Charlie's musical contribution helped establish the emotional tone of the song.
RAPPER • PERFORMER

Wiz Khalifa

For this creative presentation, I am telling the story from Wiz Khalifa's point of view. I brought the rap performance to the track and helped turn the song into a meeting point between hip-hop and pop.

I wanted my part to feel like a message about friendship, memories and seeing someone again.
03 / WHY IT WORKED

A Song About Family

The song matched the emotional purpose of the movie's final moments because its central ideas are universal.

Friendship

Remembering people who changed your life.

Family

The Fast & Furious story is built around chosen family.

Goodbye

A farewell can be emotional while memories remain.

Memories

Looking back at moments shared with someone important.

Hope

The title points toward reunion rather than only an ending.

Legacy

A song can keep a moment and its message alive.
