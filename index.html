THE FINAL RIDE

HOW THE SONG WAS CHOSEN

A SONG FOR A FINAL GOODBYE

During the production of Fast & Furious 7, the filmmakers needed a song that could capture the emotions of the movie's ending.

The song needed to represent friendship, family, memories and saying goodbye to someone who had become an important part of the story.

Charlie Puth and Wiz Khalifa came together to create "See You Again". Charlie Puth created the emotional musical foundation while Wiz Khalifa added the rap verses.

The result became much more than just a soundtrack. It became a tribute to friendship, family and memories.

THE ARTISTS

CHARLIE PUTH & WIZ KHALIFA

01

CHARLIE PUTH

Charlie Puth brought the emotional piano melody and powerful chorus to "See You Again".

His contribution helped give the song its emotional and memorable sound.

02

WIZ KHALIFA

Wiz Khalifa brought the rap verses and helped transform the song into a powerful hip-hop tribute.

His performance connected the song to the Fast & Furious world while keeping its emotional message.
